Mu2e Components

Production Solenoid (PS)
8 kW, 8 GeV protons → pions → muons
4.6T peak field
Transport Solenoid (TS)
S-shaped, selects μ− to stopping target
2.5T → 2.0T gradient
Detector Solenoid (DS)
Tracker + calorimeter + CRV
2.0T → 1.0T graded field
Stopping Target
37 Al foils (105 µm), μ− capture → e− conversion
Straw Tube Tracker
20,736 straws, 18 stations, 3.3m
σp ~ 180 keV/c
Calorimeter
1,348 CsI crystals
2 annular disks, 674 each
Cosmic Ray Veto (CRV)
5,344 scintillator counters, 4 layers
Efficiency: > 99.99%
8 GeV Proton Beam
Total POT (design): 3.6 × 1020
Muon Rate
3 × 1010 μ/sec at stopping target
